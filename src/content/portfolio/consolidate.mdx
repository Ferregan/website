<Description>
__consolidate__ releases its sixth software version—born from collective work with programmers, UI/UX designers, marketing, and others, and given its visual voice through a new campaign and illustration system. The characters, carefree and far from any spontaneous combustion, inhabit workplaces serene and good-natured, rejoicing in the simple fact that the document, the task, and the deadline are all in safe hands with this software.
